Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Infinite Builds Studio operates this store and website, including all related information, content, features, tools and products, in order to provide you, the customer, with a curated shopping experience (the "Services"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and disclose your personal information when you visit, use, or make a purchase using the Services.

Personal Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with the Services, we may collect:

  • Contact details — name, address, billing/shipping address, phone number, email address.
  • Payment information — processed securely by Stripe; we do not store your full card details on our own servers.
  • Order information — products purchased, order history, customer service communications.
  • Device/usage information — collected automatically via our hosting (Cloudflare) for security and analytics purposes.

How We Use Your Information

We use personal information to: fulfill and manage your orders, communicate with you about your order, send you marketing communications (only if you opt in), and improve our Services.

Sharing Your Information

We share information with service providers who help us operate our business, specifically:

  • Stripe — payment processing
  • Cloudflare — website hosting and security
  • Shipping carriers — to deliver your order

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Contact us via our Contact page to make a request.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact page.
