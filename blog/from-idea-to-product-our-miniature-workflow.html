From Idea to Product: Our Miniature Workflow

How Infinite Builds Turns Concepts into Real Miniatures

Creating a miniature product is more than just 3D printing — it’s a full process of design, testing, refining, and storytelling.

💡 1. It Starts with an Idea

Focus on everyday objects (chairs, kitchen sets, planters), aesthetic decor (soft shapes, minimal style), and functional miniature pieces — something small but meaningful and visually satisfying.

🎨 2. Designing in 3D (CAD Stage)

Using CAD software (like Fusion 360): correct scale (usually 1:12), smooth soft shapes, print-friendly geometry, proper thickness to avoid breaking.

🖨 3. First Prototype (3D Printing)

Testing proportions in real life, surface quality, stability, and assembly. A design that looks perfect on screen can feel “off” in real life — most adjustments happen here.

🔧 4. Refinement & Iteration

Adjust dimensions, improve curves, fix weak points, re-print multiple versions until the product looks right, feels right, and prints consistently.

🌟 5. Final Product & Styling

Clean, minimal setups, soft lighting (natural shadows), real-life context (desk/dollhouse) — helps customers visualize how the product fits into their space.

📦 6. From Workshop to Customer

Printed in small batches, checked for quality manually, packed carefully.

🧠 What Makes Our Workflow Different?

Infinite Builds designs everything in-house, tests every product physically, focuses on both aesthetic and usability, and builds for real miniature lovers.

🌿 Final Thoughts

From idea → CAD → prototype → final product, every step is intentional.
