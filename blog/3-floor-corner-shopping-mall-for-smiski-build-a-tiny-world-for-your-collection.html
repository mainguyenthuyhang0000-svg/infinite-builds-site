3-Floor Corner Shopping Mall for SMISKI: Build a Tiny World for Your Collection

A Miniature Art Toy Display House with Supermarket, Fast Food, Hotpot, Bookstore & Coffee Shop

Collecting SMISKI is only part of the fun. The way each figure is displayed can completely change how a collection feels. Instead of placing figures in a simple row on a shelf, a miniature environment gives every pose a setting, a purpose, and its own tiny story.

The 3-Floor Corner Shopping Mall for SMISKI by Infinite Builds turns a collection into an entire miniature destination. Designed as a three-story corner building, this display house combines multiple everyday spaces—including a supermarket, fast food restaurant, hotpot restaurant, bookstore, and coffee shop—inside one detailed miniature world.

With multiple rooms, miniature furniture, spiral staircases, and integrated lighting, the shopping mall creates countless possibilities for arranging and photographing your favorite figures.

More Than a SMISKI Display Shelf

A traditional display shelf focuses on organizing collectibles. The 3-Floor Corner Shopping Mall takes a different approach: it creates an environment around them.

Each floor is divided into recognizable spaces inspired by places we visit in everyday life. Instead of simply standing on a platform, SMISKI figures can browse supermarket shelves, sit inside a restaurant, explore a bookstore, or enjoy a quiet moment at a miniature coffee shop.

The result is part SMISKI display house, part miniature dollhouse, and part decorative art piece.

Three Floors, Five Miniature Worlds

Ground Floor: Miniature Supermarket

The ground floor recreates a tiny supermarket complete with counters, shelving, shopping carts, baskets, and display areas. The open layout provides plenty of room for posing different figures throughout the scene.

First Floor: Fast Food & Hotpot Restaurant

Move upstairs and the atmosphere changes completely. One section is inspired by a miniature fast food restaurant with booth-style seating; the other becomes a miniature hotpot restaurant with dining furniture and shelving that create a more social setting.

Second Floor: Bookstore & Coffee Shop

At the top, the environment becomes quieter and cozier. The miniature bookstore features bookshelves and a counter area, and the coffee shop next door offers tables, stools, and a café-inspired interior.

A Corner Design That Creates More Display Space

The L-shaped corner layout forms an immersive interior viewable from the front and different angles, creating additional visual depth. It’s particularly suitable for desks, bookshelves, display cabinets, collector shelves, creative workspaces, and bedroom décor setups.

Spiral Staircases Connect the Tiny World

Miniature spiral staircases connect the three levels, helping the shopping mall feel like one connected environment rather than six separate display boxes, and adding storytelling possibilities as figures “move” between floors.

Integrated Lighting Makes Every Floor Come Alive

Individual lights throughout the interior illuminate the supermarket, restaurants, bookstore, and coffee shop — especially fitting for SMISKI figures, whose glow-in-the-dark appearance becomes part of the atmosphere. During the day it’s a detailed collectible display; at night, an illuminated miniature world.

Designed for Creative SMISKI Display Ideas

A multi-room display gives collectors more freedom to find environments that complement different figure poses — sitting, hiding, standing, interacting — with no single correct arrangement. The shopping mall becomes a backdrop for creating your own miniature stories.

Miniature Furniture Adds Another Layer of Detail

Shelving units, tables, chairs, restaurant booths, counters, display racks, shopping carts, baskets, stools, and bookcases help visually distinguish each business and give collectors more surfaces for posing figures.

A Display Piece for Art Toy Collectors

The miniature architecture allows the display itself to become part of a collection — suitable for collectors who enjoy miniature photography, desk décor, dollhouse-style scenes, and creative art toy displays, and can serve as a centerpiece around which a larger collection grows.

From Collecting Figures to Building Tiny Stories

A figure alone represents a pose. Place that same figure behind a miniature counter, beside a shopping cart, or inside a tiny bookstore, and the pose becomes part of a scene. As new figures join a collection, the scenes can change with them.

3-Floor Corner Shopping Mall by Infinite Builds

With a Supermarket, Fast Food Restaurant, Hotpot Restaurant, Bookstore, and Coffee Shop, collectors have five distinct environments to explore and customize across three floors. Whether displayed on a desk, bookshelf, or dedicated collector space, it offers a complete miniature world for your collection.

Welcome to the tiny mall—your SMISKI collection has places to go.
