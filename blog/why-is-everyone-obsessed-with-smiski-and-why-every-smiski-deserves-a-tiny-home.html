Why Is Everyone Obsessed with Smiski? And Why Every Smiski Deserves a Tiny Home

If you’ve spent any time on TikTok, Instagram, or Reddit lately, you’ve probably seen a little green figure peeking out from behind a plant, sitting on a bookshelf, or hiding in the corner of someone’s desk. That’s Smiski — tiny glow-in-the-dark figures that have become part of people’s daily spaces.

The Joy of Collecting Smiski

Every figure has its own personality — relaxing, reading, climbing, or just staring into space. Collectors love creating little scenes that tell stories: a bookshelf becomes a tiny apartment, a desk becomes a peaceful park, a windowsill turns into a miniature café.

Build a Tiny World Instead of Just Displaying Figures

At Infinite Builds, we design 3D printed miniature displays that turn Smiski into the main character of its own tiny world:

  • 🍵 Smiski Tea House Shelf — a peaceful Asian-inspired tea house, perfect for relaxing desk setups and Japanese/Asian aesthetics.
  • 🏢 Giant Smiski Mansion Shelf — a 5-tier apartment building where each room becomes a new story.
  • 🍲 Mini Chinese Hot Pot Set — a circular dining set for group hot pot night scenes.
  • 🪜 Mini Staircase Wall Shelf — a floating staircase for a playful vertical display.
  • 🚒 Corner Fire Escape Shelf — inspired by classic city fire escapes, gives an urban apartment vibe.
  • 🧊 Hexagon Display Shelves — simple, modern, modular wall displays.

While designed with Smiski in mind, these also work for Sonny Angel, Hippers, blind box collections, mini figures, and dollhouse accessories.

More Than Display Shelves

Infinite Builds also creates dollhouse furniture & miniatures, DIY miniature kits, custom 3D printed displays, and bulk 3D printing for shops, creators, and businesses.

Build Your Tiny World

Collecting doesn’t stop after opening the blind box — that’s where the fun begins. Give your Smiski a home, build them a neighborhood, create a tiny world that makes you smile every time you walk by.
