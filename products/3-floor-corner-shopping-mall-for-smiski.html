3-Floor Corner Shopping Mall for SMISKI

$129.90 $194.85 Sale 33%

SKU: SM01

The 3-Floor Corner Shopping Mall is a large miniature display house designed to turn your SMISKI and other small art toy collection into a tiny shopping world.

Instead of a traditional figure shelf, each floor features a different shopping and dining environment with miniature furniture, counters, shelving and interior details. The open corner layout makes it easy to arrange figures throughout the building while keeping the individual rooms visible from the front.

The mall includes three themed floors with six miniature spaces:

Ground Floor – Supermarket
A miniature grocery store environment featuring display shelving, cabinets, checkout-style furniture and shopping details.

First Floor – Fast Food & Hotpot
One side features a miniature fast-food restaurant with booth seating and tables, while the other recreates a small hotpot restaurant with dining furniture and shelving.

Second Floor – Book Store & Coffee Shop
The top level combines a miniature bookstore with bookshelves and counter furniture alongside a cozy coffee shop with stools and café-style furnishings.

A central spiral staircase visually connects the different floors, while miniature ceiling lights add a warm glow throughout the mall.

SMISKI figures shown in the photos are for display purposes only and are not included.
