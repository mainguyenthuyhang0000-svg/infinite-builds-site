Compact Arch Desk Station is a multifunctional 3D printed desk organizer designed to combine a headphone stand, collectible figure display shelf, pen holder, and storage drawer in one compact setup.

The arch-shaped frame provides a dedicated place for hanging headphones, while the center platform and base create display areas for small collectible figures.
