Our Studio

The Machines Behind Every Piece

Every miniature we sell is made in our own studio in Ho Chi Minh City: printed, cut and finished by our team, never outsourced.

3D Printing Area — Eight FDM printers, numbered 1 to 8, run through the day on their own rack. Each miniature is built layer by layer so the small details stay crisp, from dollhouse furniture to planters and display pieces.

Laser Cutting Area — A large-format laser cutter shapes and engraves wood, acrylic and paper: the panels, frames and booknook parts that give our sets their structure.
